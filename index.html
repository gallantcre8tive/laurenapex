Lauren Simmons

Founder inspiration · Market pioneer

Youngest full-time trader on the NYSE (2017) and only the second African American woman to hold that position in the exchange’s history.

Lauren Simmons is an American financial expert, entrepreneur, author, speaker, and former stock trader who made history in 2017 when, at just 22 years old, she became the youngest full-time trader on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and only the second African American woman to hold that position in the exchange’s history.

Originally from Marietta, Georgia, she graduated from Kennesaw State University with a degree in genetics before pursuing a career in finance. Through determination and networking, she secured a role with Rosenblatt Securities, where she quickly gained recognition for breaking barriers in the male-dominated world of Wall Street.

After leaving the NYSE, Simmons transitioned into entrepreneurship, media, and financial education. She has become a prominent advocate for financial literacy, wealth building, and diversity in the financial industry. As an author, speaker, podcast host, and producer, she continues to inspire young professionals, women, and minorities to pursue financial independence and career excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about accounts, deposits, and how the platform works.

A professional portfolio platform where clients monitor balances, review transactions, and submit deposit or withdrawal requests. An admin reviews funding requests before balances update.
Choose a supported method (USDT TRC20 / ERC20 or BTC), send funds to the displayed wallet address, then submit your request in the app. Status stays Pending until an admin confirms. After approval, invested capital and balances update on your account.
New accounts always start at $0 — no holdings, no withdrawable balance. Funds and positions appear only after a confirmed deposit or an admin assignment.
After registration you can sign in with your email and password and use the client dashboard immediately.
